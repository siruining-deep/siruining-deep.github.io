Teaching & service

At Oxford, I taught and tutored courses including electromagnetism, quantum mechanics, relativity, mathematical methods, and quantum field theory. I have also mentored undergraduate and graduate students at Berkeley and Oxford.

I serve as President of the Berkeley Postdoc Association (2025–2027) and co-organized the QB3–Berkeley postdoc seminar series in 2025–2026. I referee for journals including Physical Review Letters, Physical Review D, and the Journal of High Energy Physics.
