I am a Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of California, Berkeley, working with Karthik Shekhar and Kranthi Mandadapu in Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering and the California Institute for Quantitative Biosciences (QB3).

My current research is in biophysics and neuroscience, with a focus on the electromechanics of biological membranes and the voltage dynamics generated by ion channel currents. My background is in theoretical physics, including holography, quantum gravity, and string compactification.

I received my DPhil in theoretical physics from the University of Oxford in 2024, supervised by Joseph Conlon.
